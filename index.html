Standard Operating Survival Guidelines

Actionable, expert-verified checklists for immediate protection before, during, and after severe crisis events.

1. Drop, Cover & Hold On

  • Drop onto your hands and knees immediately to avoid being knocked down.
  • Cover your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk.
  • Hold on until the shaking stops. Stay away from glass windows and heavy fixtures.

2. If Indoors or Driving

  • DO NOT use elevators under any circumstances.
  • If in bed, hold on and stay there, protecting your head with a pillow.
  • If driving, pull over safely to an open area away from overpasses and poles.

3. Post-Shaking Evacuation

  • Check for gas leaks and smell of smoke; shut off main utility valves.
  • Follow GeoShield AI safe routes away from damaged building facades.
  • Prepare for aftershocks; tune into official broadcast alerts.

1. Immediate Action

  • Move immediately to higher ground or upper floor; do not delay.
  • Do NOT walk, swim, or drive through moving floodwaters ("Turn Around, Don't Drown").
  • Just 15 cm of moving water can knock you down, 30 cm can float a vehicle.

2. Utility Shutdown

  • Disconnect all electrical appliances and switch off main circuit breakers.
  • Avoid touching any electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in water.
  • Keep essential emergency medicine and documents sealed in waterproof bags.

3. Water & Food Safety

  • Boil drinking water or use purification tablets; flood water carries pathogens.
  • Avoid consuming any food that came into contact with floodwater.
  • Signal for rescue from the highest visible roof point, never from closed attics.

1. Prior to Landfall

  • Board up or tape large glass windows; secure loose outdoor objects.
  • Keep emergency batteries, power banks, and radio charged.
  • Evacuate coastal lowlands to designated reinforced storm shelters.

2. During the Storm

  • Stay indoors in the strongest central room without windows.
  • Beware of the "Eye of the Storm" (calm lull followed by sudden reverse winds).
  • Listen continuously to official weather advisories on your mobile phone.

3. Post-Cyclone Protocol

  • Watch for downed power lines and report live wires to emergency teams.
  • Do not enter damaged buildings until certified safe by civil inspectors.
  • Cooperate with relief teams and keep phone lines open for emergency calls.

1. Evacuation & Smoke

  • Crawl low under smoke toward the nearest marked fire exit.
  • Feel doors with the back of your hand before opening; if hot, find another exit.
  • Never use elevators during a structural fire emergency.

2. Stop, Drop & Roll

  • If your clothes catch fire: Stop immediately, Drop to the floor, and Roll.
  • Smother flames with a blanket or heavy coat if available.
  • Cool minor burns immediately with clean, cool running water.

3. Once Outside

  • Stay outside at the pre-designated assembly point; NEVER go back inside.
  • Call 101 / 112 immediately with your exact building sector coordinates.
  • Inform firefighters if anyone remains trapped inside.
